A few years have passed since I last wrote. A lot has happened since and I now feel I have a better understanding of occurrences around me, as well as myself.

I'll make this short: during my annual review of 2025, I have noticed some patterns that appear throughout my annual reviews, dating back to 2022, when I first started doing them (as far as I can find evidence).

One of the patterns is I feel better when I create, and creating through writing is one of _my_ creative expressions.

Moreover, I'm one of those people who fall into the following categories:
* I discount things I achieved and how valuable they are (however small they might be; as long as they make me feel accomplished)
* I consume a lot and forget what (and how to synthesize it).

Both have an attached network effect: I, as well as other people around me, are not aware of my interests and projects (and learnings from them), so I miss out on opportunities to meet new people, spark new conversations, help others, and gain something from it myself. 
On more than one occasion this year, knowledge I deemed "obvious" and felt intuitively, helped and intrigued others in ways I couldn't have imagined on my own.

For these reasons, I have decided to share:
* learnings from what I consume and
* projects I'm working on
throughout 2026.

I invite anyone who might stumble upon this website and share similar interests or have the same questions, to reach out.
