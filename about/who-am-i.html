Who am I

I build products, lately with a lot of AI. This site is where I write down what I learn, so that I do not forget it.

I'm Kristina. I work in product. I spent a bit over a year on the revenue and growth side, where I ran marketing and revenue operations for the first time and led a marketing team. In 2026 I moved back to product. Being a product builder right now is the most exciting thing I can imagine doing.

What I do

  • Product. At the moment: bringing product analytics to a team that never had it, and running a CRM migration.
  • AI in everyday work. I build small apps with AI (the latest one outside of work is focustogether.online), I automated a big business process with AI at work, and I teach colleagues how to use AI in their day-to-day work.
  • Writing. A newsletter at whatishappen.ing, and this site.

Why this site exists

Every year I do an annual review, and the same two patterns keep coming back. I discount what I achieve, and I consume a lot and forget most of it. Writing helps with both. It also tells other people what I care about, and things I thought were obvious have helped and interested people more than once. More in Rhyming.

Outside work

Ceramics classes with my mom. Trail running: my first Spartan race, and the Life on Mars trail race. Pickleball. And a lot of history in books, films and documentaries. My current rabbit hole is Afghanistan, through Ghost Wars by Steve Coll.

Say hi

If you have the same interests or the same questions, write to me on X or LinkedIn. I went through the mentorship programme in the Lenny's Newsletter community, and I want to be a mentor there too.
